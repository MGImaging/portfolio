Spaces Media: 24/7 X Spaces radio automation Founder

spaces.media · @Spaces_MediaLLC

An AI operations agent keeps a round-the-clock Web3 radio station live on X Spaces, recovers from drops and crashes, and hands each show to the next host on time.

How it works
Problem
A Web3 radio station on X Spaces that runs around the clock with 40+ shows a week. Live Spaces drop, clients crash, and every show has to be handed to the next host on time. Doing that by hand day and night isn't sustainable.
What I built
  • An AI operations agent ("Orion", built on Hermes Agent) that manages the live Space on a Windows desktop, with Discord as its control and alert channel.
  • Keep-alive: it detects a lost broadcast, goes live again after 1 minute down, and retries every 2 minutes.
  • Crash recovery, plus show-to-show host handoffs.
  • Schedule management: a daily 6 a.m. schedule post, public warnings and host DMs 30 and 5 minutes before each show, and a title change for each show.
  • The Spaces Media website, built with AI coding agents.
Controls
  • A handoff counts as done only when the next host is confirmed as co-host in the room. A click that looks right isn't enough.
  • Routine health checks run as plain scripts with no AI model cost.
  • Dangerous commands need my approval, and credentials stay in a vault the agent can't read.
  • Every incident becomes a written procedure.
Results
The station broadcasts 24/7, runs 40+ shows a week, has thousands of hours on air, and has about 5.4k followers on X.

How it works
Problem
Trading-bot sellers post screenshots, not audited results. Nobody showed what prediction-market bots actually keep after fees.
What I built
  • A live leaderboard of about 133 Polymarket trading bots, scored from each wallet's public on-chain history.

Results
Live and public. Data last refreshed Oct 19, 2026.

A disciplined AI paper-trading research system

Alpaca paper-trading API · research and paper trading only, no real money

37pre-registered strategies
0showed an edge
Paperonly, no real money

An end-to-end options research system with hard risk limits, built to find out honestly whether a strategy has an edge before any money is at risk.

How it works
Problem
Test whether options strategies have a real edge, without fooling myself, before any money is at risk.
What I built
Working with AI coding agents, I built an end-to-end system: a weekly universe build, an options-flow scanner, defined-risk spread construction, scheduled trade management, end-of-day reports, and an automated daily review that proposes fixes. A daily pre-market research briefing written by a separate research agent feeds into it.
Controls
  • Paper account only, enforced in code.
  • A kill switch: a 6% drawdown halts new trades until I approve a restart, and there's a 2% daily-loss stop and a pause when data fails.
  • At most 20 orders a day, with no market orders and no uncovered shorts.
  • Every decision is logged with the reasons behind it.
  • Change control: risk limits can never be loosened, and parameters change at most once a day.
Results
37 strategies tested, all pre-registered, with sealed holdout data, out-of-sample splits, deflated Sharpe ratios and double-cost stress tests. None showed an edge, so the holdouts stayed sealed and I reported the result as-is instead of curve-fitting. Paper trading began Oct 19, 2026 to collect forward data. No profits are claimed.

Multi-agent operations for Michael Goss Imaging

michaelgossimaging.com · exterior 3D landscape and outdoor-living renderings, built in Structure Studios VIP 3D

A team of specialist agents handles research, outreach, follow-up and records for a solo studio, with nothing sent or spent without my approval.

How it works
Problem
I'm a solo operator who needs research, outreach, follow-up and record-keeping done without hiring staff, and without risking spam or sending the wrong thing.
What I built
  • An orchestrator agent that routes work to specialist agents for market research, lead research, web design, email and records.
  • A cloud agent linked to a self-hosted desktop email agent through two-way messaging and authenticated webhooks.
  • A daily operations brief.
Controls
  • Each agent has one job and written authority limits. Nothing is sent and no money is spent without my approval.
  • Contacts are verified on company websites only, never guessed.
  • Opt-out scanning, a bounce circuit breaker, a single do-not-contact list, and a no-marketing rule for existing clients.
Results
A compliant, logged outreach system with these controls built in:
  • Verified contacts only: each address is confirmed on the company's own website, never guessed or bulk-scraped.
  • Do-not-contact enforcement: a single list that every agent checks before drafting, queuing or sending.
  • Approval before sending: outbound mail goes out only after my sign-off.
  • Periodic audits: I review the outreach system's sends, bounces, opt-outs and configuration, and fixes ship only after I approve them.

How I work

The same habits I use in welding: work to code, check the work, and don't sign off on something you haven't inspected.

Spec first

Every system starts as a written specification: goals, inputs, success checks, and what the agent must never do.

Safety policies

Hard limits are written down and enforced in code: kill switches, caps, do-not-contact lists, and paper-only checks.

Human approval

Anything that sends, spends, or can't be undone waits for my OK.

Logging

Every decision and incident is recorded, so I can see why something happened and fix it.

Honest evaluation

Tests are pre-registered, holdouts stay sealed, and I report results as-is, including when the answer is "no edge."

AI-assisted development

I design the system and direct AI coding agents, then read, test and run the resulting code.
